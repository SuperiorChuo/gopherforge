# AI Visibility

The **visibility service** tracks how brands surface in AI search and answers: keywords, SERP checks and trend dashboards.

## Capabilities

- **Overview**: visibility score, brand/keyword coverage, historical trends.
- **Brands & keywords**: brand profiles, keyword libraries, check plans.
- **SERP checks**: result-page fetches and check records with historical comparison.
- **Dashboards**: per brand / keyword / period visualization and export.
- **Data scope**: tenant-isolated, role-gated (`visibility:*` permissions).

## Boundaries

- 🧪 Experimental line: APIs and schemas may still change.
- Reachability and quotas of checked sources depend on external environments; external data-source credentials are user-configured.
